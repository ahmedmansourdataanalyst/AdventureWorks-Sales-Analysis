# AdventureWorks Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyzes AdventureWorks sales data using Microsoft Power BI to evaluate business performance, monitor key metrics, and uncover insights into sales trends, product performance, and customer behavior.

## Objectives
- Monitor key business performance indicators, including revenue, profit, orders, and returns.
- Analyze sales performance across products and categories.
- Evaluate customer behavior and identify top-performing customers.
- Track revenue and order trends over time.
- Compare actual performance against business targets.
- Develop interactive dashboards to support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Modeling
- Data Visualization
- KPI Development

## Key Metrics
Total Revenue: 24,914,587 $

Total Profit: 10,457,715 $

## Key Insights

- **Strong Overall Performance:** Generated $24.9M in revenue and $10.5M in profit from 25.2K orders, with an approximate profit margin of 42.2%.
- **Low Return Rate:** Maintained an overall return rate of just 2.2%, indicating a relatively low level of product returns.
- **Revenue Growth:** Monthly revenue increased by 3.31% compared to the previous month, reaching $1.83M.
- **Category Performance:** Accessories recorded 17K orders, followed by Bikes with 13.9K and Clothing with 7K.
- **Top-Selling Products:** Water Bottle - 30 oz. led product orders with 3,983, followed by Patch Kit/8 Patches with 2,952 orders.
- **Product Returns:** Sport-100 Helmet (Red) had a 3.33% return rate, the highest among the top 10 products displayed.
- **Customer and Product Analysis:** Identified purchasing patterns and product performance through interactive Power BI visualizations and dynamic metrics.
